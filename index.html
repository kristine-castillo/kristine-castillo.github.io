01 / Introduction

ACADEMIC QUALIFICATIONS

I am an educator with a background in Information Technology and a Master of Arts in English as a Second Language (MA-ESL). I have three years of teaching experience at the University of the Cordilleras handling subjects such as English, Social Sciences, Popular Culture, and Spanish as a foreign language. Moreover, I have 13 years of experience teaching English to speakers of other languages with specialty in Basic and Advaned English, TOEIC Speaking, Business English and English for Specific Purposes.

I am passionate about education, communication, and helping students develop their knowledge and skills. My academic background in IT and language education has shaped my creative, adaptable, and student-centered approach to learning.
